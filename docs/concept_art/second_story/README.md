# Second Story concept art

This board set explores the second story as a lived-in social space: tenants have distinct routines and pressures, apartments show different levels of care, and shared spaces create both friction and mutual aid.

## Direction to carry into implementation

- Keep the warm terracotta / peach / plum palette and chunky painterly forms used by the existing title art.
- Make condition readable through practical details: light, leaks, storage, safe flooring, working fixtures, and tenant-made objects.
- Preserve tenant dignity. Improvement should mean safety, comfort, and agency rather than luxury or uniformity.
- Use shared spaces as story surfaces where repairs, noise, privacy, and neighbor relationships intersect.
